Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Don’t see yours? Text us and a person will answer.

How do I avoid running short halfway through a job?

Order everything at once, plus your waste. Same lot, same color, all delivered together. Different lots mean mismatched floors, and to a homeowner that always shows.

What’s the number one mistake when ordering flooring?

Ordering short just to save a few bucks. The next batch can be a different lot that doesn’t match. Order once, order right, and add your 10%.

Do you install flooring?

No. Jovic is supply only. We provide the material and your crew handles the install.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across San Diego and north to Oceanside. Working farther out, up to Temecula? Ask us for an estimate.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 7am to 5pm. Deliveries run 8am to 5pm. We’re closed on Sundays.

How do I get a price?

Contractor pricing depends on the job, so we quote directly. Call, text or send the form and we’ll get back to you.

How can I pay?

We accept Zelle, card and financing. We’ll confirm the options when we quote your job.

Cabinets, trim and baseboards, shower doors, quartz countertops, vanities and closet built-ins. Ask if you need something not listed.
